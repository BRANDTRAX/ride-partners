The technology behind independent local transport.

Ride Partners LLC licenses a complete dispatch and booking platform to independent, locally licensed transport operators. You keep your brand, your drivers and your market. We provide the technology.

Ride Partners LLC provides software and technology only. It does not provide, arrange or control transport services. All local rides, dispatch services and operational logistics are independently fulfilled by licensed local operating partners, who are solely responsible for the transport services they provide.

What we do

Ride Partners LLC is a US-based technology and software platform provider. We license our digital infrastructure to independent local transport operators and provide a comprehensive, streamlined dispatch solution — fully set up, supported and maintained by us, so operators can run a modern, app-based service without building the technology themselves.
